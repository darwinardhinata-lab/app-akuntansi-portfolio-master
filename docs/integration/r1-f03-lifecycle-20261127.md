## Outbound

- `warehouse.outbound` exposes list/create/store, not a dedicated update or cancel action. Ordinary journal edit, delete and import replacement reject the automatic `Outbound` journal. This does not prove all legacy source-reference writers safe.
- The authenticated `so.rollback` route used an invoice reference alone to select journals and stock movements. The normal manual outbound route accepts that reference and an IDR3 debit offset, without aliasing the bank and inventory accounts. In the disposable test, both source documents were created via their normal HTTP routes, and the normal Maker action established a `400.00` hold. The unpatched rollback removed the separate outbound's `100.01` bank debit: actual `500.00 → 399.99`, available `100.00 → −0.01`, hold unchanged, read from a separate connection.
- The local candidate serializes manual outbound and SO rollback under the existing financial fence. Rollback locks the selected records, rejects ambiguous journal or movement provenance before writes, and checks the net removal of projected GL against bank capacity. It deletes only the checked movement IDs. Legacy shipments without the expected source links or movement provenance may require manual review; this is a fail-closed rule, not a historical repair.

## Purchase-return reversal: source review only

`purchase-returns.reverse` is route-registered but requires A2, enabled bank workflow and posting intents, a permitted Finance actor and an owned PO. The normal original credits configured inventory and debits configured trade AP or supplier advance. For an eligible two-line reversal, the only credit is the original debit, which must equal the single unpaid bill's credit COA. The AP source check additionally requires a credit-normal recognition account. With separate AP and normal-debit IDR3 mappings, the reversal cannot credit IDR3. No admissible normal flow to a protected bank decrease was demonstrated; no MariaDB bank-impact test or patch was made for this route. The runtime mapping resolver does not itself prove bank/AP disjointness; a mis-mapped or historical account is not cleared by this conclusion. No operational mapping or DB was inspected or changed.

Opening debit replacement remains a separate [Accounting decision](r1-f03-opening-debit-decision-20261127.md). No staging, main DB, production flags, operational fixtures or journal history were modified.
